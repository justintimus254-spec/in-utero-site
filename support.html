Common questions

I bought in-utero Full on another phone. How do I get it here?
Sign in with the same Apple ID, open Profile and tap Restore purchase.

How do I change or remove my due date?
Profile → Remove due date, then enter the new one. It is stored only on your phone.

Can I get a refund?
Purchases are handled by Apple. Request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Is this medical advice?
No. The 3D models are illustrations, not a picture of your baby. For anything about your health or pregnancy, talk to your doctor or midwife.
