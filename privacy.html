in-utero — Privacy Policy

Effective: November 4, 2026

in-utero is published by Justin Timus. Questions about this policy: justintimus254@gmail.com.

The short version

in-utero keeps what you enter on your phone. We don't have accounts, we don't run analytics or ads, and we don't collect, sell or share your information.

What the app stores, and where

  • Your due date (or the date you dated your pregnancy from: last period, scan or embryo transfer). It is saved in the app's own storage on your phone so Home and the Baby screen can show where you are today. It is never sent to us or anyone else. Remove it any time from Profile → Remove due date, or by deleting the app.
  • Face ID / Touch ID / passcode. The app asks your phone to confirm it's you each time it opens. That check is done entirely by iOS: the app only learns "yes" or "no" and never sees or stores your face, fingerprint or passcode.
  • in-utero Full purchase. Buying and restoring go through Apple's App Store. Apple handles payment; the app only learns whether this Apple ID owns in-utero Full, and remembers that on the phone. We never see your name, Apple ID or payment details.

What we don't do

  • No account or sign-in.
  • No analytics, tracking, advertising or advertising identifiers.
  • No location, camera, microphone, contacts or photos access.
  • Nothing you enter leaves your phone.

App updates

The app checks for updates to its own content from Expo (expo.dev), the service it is built with, when it opens. That request carries technical details needed to deliver the right update (such as the app version and the phone's platform) and nothing you have entered. See Expo's privacy policy at https://expo.dev/privacy.

Children

in-utero is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect information from anyone.

Not medical advice

in-utero is for education and curiosity. The 3D models are illustrations, not a picture of your baby, and the app is not a medical device. For anything about your health or your pregnancy, talk to your doctor or midwife.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at this address with a new effective date. If the app ever starts collecting information (for example, optional accounts), this policy will be updated first.
